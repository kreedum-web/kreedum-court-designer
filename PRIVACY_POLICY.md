# Privacy Policy – Kreedum Construction app

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Kreedum Construction ("we") built the Kreedum Construction app as a free design
tool for visualising sports courts and playground flooring.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app has no accounts, no login, no analytics,
no advertising and no internet access of its own.

**Photos / logos:** if you choose to add a logo, the app reads only the image you
pick from your gallery. It is used on your device to draw the design and is not
uploaded anywhere.

**Sharing and enquiries:** when you tap Share, WhatsApp, Call or Email, the app
hands the design (image, PDF and/or a text summary) to the app you choose.
Anything you then send is handled by that app and by us as a normal business
enquiry, and is not stored by this app.

**Children:** the app is a general audience business tool and does not knowingly
collect information from anyone.
